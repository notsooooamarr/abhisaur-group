FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer free consultations? ◆
Yes! We offer a completely free initial consultation — no commitment required. We'll discuss your project, understand your vision and budget, and suggest the best approach.
What is your project timeline? ◆
Timeline depends on project scope. Interior design projects: 15–45 days. Full construction: 3–12 months. We always provide a clear timeline upfront before starting.
Do you work outside Rewa? ◆
Yes, we serve clients across Madhya Pradesh including Satna, Sidhi, Shahdol, and surrounding districts. Contact us to discuss your location.
What is your minimum project budget? ◆
We work with all budget ranges. Whether it's a single room interior or a full home construction, we tailor our services to your budget without compromising on quality.
Do you provide 3D designs before starting? ◆
Absolutely. For most projects, we create detailed 3D renders and floor plans for your approval before any construction or installation begins.
What materials do you use? ◆
We use quality-approved materials from trusted suppliers. We'll recommend options at different price points so you can make an informed choice for your budget.
